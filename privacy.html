Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

SleepSphere does not collect your data. There is no account, no server and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your own device unless you deliberately export it.

The short version. Your sleep entries, plans and reflections are stored only in the app on your phone. They are never uploaded, never sold, never shared, and never seen by us — because there is nowhere for them to go. The single exception is tester feedback, which is sent only if you tap Send, and which contains none of them.

What the app stores

SleepSphere saves what you type into it: your Bio Harmony check-ins, night plans, morning records, reflections, experiments, travel plans and settings. This is stored locally on your device using standard app storage.

We have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also erase everything from inside the app at any time, on the My records screen.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, SleepSphere reads a small number of values to save you typing them:

  • your recent activity (active minutes, active energy, steps)
  • last night's sleep, as Health recorded it
  • your cycle phase — only if you have separately switched cycle context on

SleepSphere never writes anything to Apple Health, and Health data is never transmitted off your device. It is read into the app, used to fill in a form, and that is all.

Connecting Health is entirely optional. If you decline, every feature still works — the app simply asks you for the same information instead.

Health data is never used for advertising, never shared with third parties, and never used for any purpose other than showing you your own information inside the app.

The one exception: tester feedback

If you tap Send on the feedback screen, that feedback is sent. It is the only thing in SleepSphere that leaves your device, and only when you deliberately send it.

What goes: the five ratings, the two dropdown answers and whatever you typed in the comment box. What does not go: your sleep records, times, plans, reflections, location or anything read from Apple Health. Those never leave, feedback or not.

Depending on your device it is either submitted through the site's form handler or handed to your own email app to send — in which case you can see exactly what it says, and can decide not to send it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or logins
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of data — the only thing that ever reaches us is feedback you choose to send
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking

Exports you choose to make

The app can export your records as a CSV or backup file. Once you export a file, it is yours and it leaves the app's control — where it goes next is up to you. Take the same care with it that you would with any personal health note.

SleepSphere Motherhood

Motherhood is an optional part of SleepSphere for recording rest, energy and wellbeing through pregnancy and the months after birth. It is off until you turn it on, and it collects nothing before you do.

  • Separate from everything else. Motherhood records are kept under their own storage key, never inside the app state that the study exports and the backup file are built from.
  • Encrypted on your device. When you turn the module on you choose a passcode. The records are encrypted with AES-GCM under a key derived from that passcode (PBKDF2-SHA256). What is saved on the device is ciphertext — not the dates, not the stage, not a single field name. The passcode itself is never stored, so if you forget it the records cannot be recovered.
  • Never in a research export. The study CSV is still 50 columns and carries nothing from this module — not the records, not a summary, and not a flag saying you use it. A researcher receiving your study files cannot tell whether this module is enabled on your phone.
  • Never transmitted. The module contains no network code. The only way any of it leaves the device is the export button inside the module, which you press, which saves a file to your downloads.
  • Erased when you erase. "Erase everything" clears this store too.
  • Yours to inspect. You can view, correct, export and delete every entry, and delete the whole module and its passcode, from inside it.

What the encryption does not protect against, stated plainly: while the module is open on screen the records are decrypted in memory, because that is the only way to show them to you. Someone holding your unlocked phone at that moment can read what is on screen. Lock the module or close it when you step away.

Motherhood does not diagnose anything and makes no medical promises. It keeps what you write down. It contains general information of the kind a midwife or a public-health leaflet gives, and a reference list of symptoms worth contacting someone about — that list is not generated from your entries, and the app does not monitor what you record and form an opinion about your health.

Children

SleepSphere is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Medical disclaimer

SleepSphere is a reflection and journalling tool. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any medical condition, and it does not measure sleep stages. Nothing in it is medical advice. If sleep is affecting your health, please speak to a qualified clinician.
