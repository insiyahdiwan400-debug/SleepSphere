SLEEPSPHERE V5 — GUIDED TESTER PROTOTYPE

WHAT IS NEW

- A friendly first-time welcome explains the prototype before a tester begins.
- One clear journey: Check today → Plan tonight → Record tomorrow.
- The main button changes automatically to show the tester's next step.
- Completed steps are visibly marked on the home screen.
- Advanced research tools are kept under More so the main journey stays simple.
- Share prototype creates a tester link and opens the phone's sharing menu.
- The tester link opens with ?tester=1 and shows the prototype welcome.
- Bio Harmony, the Restoration Twin, Fajr-aware planning, privacy controls,
  reflection tools, exports and offline support remain included.

HOW TO PUBLISH ON NETLIFY

1. Sign in to Netlify and open the existing SleepSphere site.
2. Choose Deploys.
3. Drag this entire SleepSphere_V5 folder into the manual deploy area.
4. Wait until Netlify says the deployment is published.
5. Open the public Netlify link on your phone and tap Share prototype.

Upload the whole folder, not index.html by itself. The supporting files provide the
install icon, offline mode and privacy/security headers.

HOW TO INVITE TESTERS

After publishing, open the Netlify version and tap Share prototype. On supported phones,
the normal sharing menu will open. Otherwise, SleepSphere copies the invitation and link.
Use that link with an Instagram Story link sticker. It will use your public Netlify address.

PRIVACY NOTE

Entries stay in each tester's own browser unless that tester deliberately exports them.
This prototype is for reflection and feedback; it is not a medical device or diagnosis.
