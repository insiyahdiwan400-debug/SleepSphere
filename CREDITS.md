## Living Sky footage

> **The footage layer is off by default.** The app opens on the drawn
> sky and downloads no video unless it is asked for, with `?sky=real` or
> `SSLivingSky.setEnabled(true)`. Two reasons: playback of these clips on
> iPhone Safari is **unverified** (see below), and the footage weighs
> 5–11.5 MB against a page that is otherwise under a megabyte. The clips
> below ship with the app and are used the moment footage is switched on.

Both clips are **CC BY 2.0**, which permits use and redistribution,
including commercially, with attribution. Attribution is shown in the app
itself — a credit line appears on screen whenever a clip is actually
playing — because the licence requires credit wherever the work appears,
and a line in a file nobody opens is not that.

Both were confirmed to be **H.264 in an MP4 container** by reading the
`avc1` entry from the sample-description box of the files themselves. The
`format` field on the source site reads "MPEG4", which names the container
and not the codec; an MPEG-4 Part 2 file would carry `mp4v` there and
would not play on an iPhone.

### Dayparts with no footage

`dawn`, `morning` and `day` have **no recording**. They are deliberately
absent from the manifest in `living-sky.js` rather than pointed at a
placeholder, so they report `unavailable` and the drawn sky shows instead.
Sourcing clips for them is outstanding work, not a solved problem.

### Considered and rejected

| Source | Why not |
|---|---|
| `TimelapseSky` (CC0, evening/sunset/sunrise) | 67.8 MB for 289s at 640×480. Far too large to bundle, and there is no transcoder in this project's toolchain to cut or re-encode it. |
| `CloudsPassingTimelapse` (Public Domain Mark) | 208 MB. Same reason. |
| `Flickr-36184302850` "Sky Animation / Timelapse" (CC BY 2.0) | Titled *Animation*. Could not be confirmed as a recording rather than CGI, and the requirement is authentic recorded footage. |
| Anything under CC BY-NC or CC BY-ND | NC forbids commercial use, which this app cannot guarantee it will never have; ND forbids the re-encoding that mobile optimisation needs. |

## Test fixtures

### `test/fixtures/playback-probe.webm`

Generated by this project, by recording a canvas gradient with
`MediaRecorder` inside the test browser. It exists because this project's
Chromium is built without H.264 and therefore cannot decode the shipped
clips at all, so the media adapter's playback path is exercised against a
format it *can* decode.

It is a moving gradient. It is not sky footage, it is not shipped with the
app, and it is not presented as a recording of anything.

## Motherhood scenery

Four scenes in `media/motherhood/`. Each is a short clip of real
footage plus a still, and the still is that clip own first frame — so
the scene you see before the video decodes is the same scene, not a
different photograph standing in for it.

All four are from Pexels under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/),
which permits free use and modification, including commercially,
without attribution. They are credited anyway, because footage someone
else shot should carry their name whether or not a licence compels it.

| Scene | Source | Photographer |
|---|---|---|
| Aurora Sanctuary | [pexels.com/video/11860943](https://www.pexels.com/video/mountain-and-aurora-borealis-11860943/) | Cristian Manieri |
| Celestial Lake | [pexels.com/video/13423306](https://www.pexels.com/video/stars-in-night-sky-over-mountain-lake-13423306/) | Dmitry Varennikov |
| First Light | [pexels.com/video/4046335](https://www.pexels.com/video/the-sun-rising-behind-the-mountains-4046335/) | Taryn Elliott |
| Moonlit Meadow | [pexels.com/video/12902487](https://www.pexels.com/video/low-angle-view-of-tall-wild-grass-and-the-moon-12902487/) | Camille Brodard |

Each was trimmed to eight or ten seconds, cropped to 9:16 and
re-encoded by this project as H.264 and VP9. Nothing was composited,
relit, reversed or speed-ramped. What moves in them is what the camera
recorded moving.

### Photographs sourced earlier, now superseded

Four Pexels stills were used before the footage existed: an aurora over
a lake by Tobias Bjorkli, a star-filled lake by JackerKun, a valley
above the cloud by Quang Nguyen Vinh, and a meadow under blue night by
Dave Love. They are no longer in the repository — each scene now
derives its still from its own clip, so that the still and the motion
can never disagree.

### Photographs that were supplied but not used

An asset patch dated 19 October 2026 supplied `aurora.webp` and
`firstlight.webp` cropped from the project owner own photographs
(IMG_5769 and IMG_5767). Both were taken through an aircraft cabin
window: the first keeps the window vignette and an aircraft wing and
winglet in the lower right, the second is a full cabin window frame
with a wing across the centre. The approved Motherhood design excludes
aircraft imagery of any kind, so neither was installed. They are not in
this repository.
